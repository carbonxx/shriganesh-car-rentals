Places to visit in Mangalore

Start with sunset at Panambur or Tannirbhavi beach, then visit the Kadri Manjunath and Mangaladevi temples. Pilikula Nisargadhama makes an easy half-day trip for families, and the old port area around Sultan Battery is great for an evening drive.

Weekend drives near Mangalore

Udupi's Krishna Temple and Malpe Beach are about an hour north. Head up the ghats to Madikeri in Coorg or to Chikmagalur for coffee estates and cool weather, or drive south along the coast into Kerala.
